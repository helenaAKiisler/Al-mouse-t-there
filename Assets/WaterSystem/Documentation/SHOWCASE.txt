Water System showcase scenes
After importing the asset package into a Unity 6.3+ URP project, choose
Water > Set Up Active URP Renderer. Render Graph must be enabled.
Open Assets/WaterSystem/Scenes/SCN_WaterShowcase_Planes.unity or
Assets/WaterSystem/Scenes/SCN_WaterShowcase_Volumes.unity.
In Play mode: 0 overview, 1-4 individual styles, right-drag orbit, scroll zoom.
Closed water meshes use the configured water-volume layer. Camera outside only.
See USER_GUIDE.md in this Documentation folder for setup and controls.
Full Unity project: https://github.com/SakanaLazy/Water_Material_Experimentation
